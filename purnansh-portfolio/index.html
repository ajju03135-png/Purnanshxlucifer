About me, 3 min read

      
From a commerce classroom to marketing a road-safety startup

      
I’m Purnansh, a B.Com student in Jaipur. On paper I study accounts and business. Most of my real learning has happened outside class: running social media for businesses, talking to customers, and working out why some content works and most doesn’t.

      

        
My first proper marketing job was as a Digital Marketing Executive at Dizivel, a marketing agency. I ran campaigns for clients, made design assets, and saw how client work really runs: briefs, deadlines, revisions, and the gap between what a client asks for and what they actually need. After that I worked with Gzenith, an e-commerce brand, which showed me the selling side of marketing.

        
Then I started LUCIPÉAK, my own creative marketing studio. I’ve designed graphics for restaurants and hotels, built websites, and managed social accounts for local businesses. Working alone means I handle everything: strategy, design, video, the client call and the delivery.

        
Right now I’m a Founder’s Office Intern at DrivoMate, a Jaipur startup building an AI road-safety system for two-wheelers. A lot of my work happens on the road. I go where delivery riders wait between orders, pitch the product, run surveys and listen. Then I come back and turn what I heard into reels, which I shoot and edit myself.

        
AI is a big part of how I work. I use it for scripts, ad concepts, visuals, research and building websites, so I can get more done without a big team.

        
A few things I now believe about marketing:

        
The person comes before the product. People stop scrolling for a face and a problem they recognise, not a feature list.

        
Real footage beats heavy effects. A shaky clip of a real rider is more believable than a polished animation.

        
The hook has to earn the first three seconds. If the opening doesn’t land, nothing after it matters.

        
Next, I want to work with a team that handles many brands, so I keep learning how different audiences respond and get better every week.
